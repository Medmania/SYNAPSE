FAQ

Do I need to login?
Yes. Login with Google is required to register for any event and get a pass.
How do I get a pass?
Login → Click “Get Pass” on an event → Fill remaining details → Pass is generated.
Can I see only my passes?
Yes. “My Passes” shows only the tickets registered with your Google account.
Where are live scores?
